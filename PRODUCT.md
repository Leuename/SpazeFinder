# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The owner-developer, on their own Windows machine, running an occasional disk-cleanup session: disk is filling up, they want to find the space hogs and act on them fast. Secondary: the app may be published on GitHub later, so it should hold up in front of other developers comparing it to WinDirStat/WizTree.

## Product Purpose

SpazeFinder scans a whole drive with admin rights and shows what takes the space as a biggest-first tree, with Explorer-like actions (open, reveal, recycle-bin delete, rename, move) so cleanup happens in place. Success: the user finds and clears the space hogs in one short session without opening Explorer.

## Positioning

The fastest path from "disk full" to "space freed" — scan, see, act, done.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, quiet. An instrument, not an experience: data leads, nothing shouts, interactions feel engineered. Elegant and simple — minimalist by intent, not by absence of design.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated dashboard look: card grids, glassmorphism, purple-blue SaaS palettes.
- Anything that reads "template" — the design must be distinguishable from AI slop at a glance.

## Design Principles

- Data is the interface: sizes, bars, and hierarchy carry the design; chrome stays out of the way.
- Monochrome discipline: a black/white base; gradient accents are the single signature element and appear only where they mean something (size bars, active states).
- Every interaction earns its motion: fast, exponential ease-out, nothing decorative.
- One screen, one job: drive pick, scanning, tree — each state is unmistakable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Sensible defaults: WCAG AA contrast for text, prefers-reduced-motion honored on all animation, keyboard-reachable primary actions. No formal compliance target.
